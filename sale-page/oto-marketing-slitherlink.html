Straight answers

Before you decide.

Do I need any other upgrade first?
No. Marketing Studio needs only your Builder. It applies to every game you have, including variants and bundles if you own those tools.
Does the Studio use AI to make the images?
No. Images and the video are drawn in your browser from your own game pages. Nothing is sent to an AI service, and nothing is uploaded.
Where do the backdrops come from?
They are drawn by the Studio itself — wood, linen, stone, chalkboard or a night sky — and picked to suit your theme, or chosen by you. There are no stock photos to license.
Will the images give away the answers?
No. The Studio leaves out the answer pages and the host’s question pages, so your listing shows the game without spoiling it.
What do I need to make the video?
A recent Chrome or Edge on a computer. The video takes about a minute to make; you can turn it off and make the images only.
