Mosaic mode

Solve the pieces. Nothing makes sense. Then it does.

Press Assemble and watch the pieces become one picture — then pick the right picture from the board to claim the word.

Try it · illustrative sample
Seven pieces, dealt in a shuffled order. The two blank corners come printed on the board.
Answer board — which picture is it?
  1. Solve each piece. Draw the loop, shade inside it. On its own, a piece shows a scrap of shape and no hint of the whole.
  2. Cut it out. Pieces print four to a page with cut lines, in a shuffled order. Each is marked “this way up”, so nothing needs rotating.
  3. Place it on the Assembly Board. On Easy and Medium each piece says where it goes (“Piece 3 · place at B1”). On Hard there are no coordinates — players match the outlines themselves.
  4. Pick the picture. The answer board shows 6 silhouettes, or 8 on Hard, with look-alikes among them — a deer next to the reindeer.
  5. Its name is the code. In a Mosaic game the picture’s name is the secret code that opens the Final Lock. In a mixed game it is one chapter’s word, alongside words spelled in letters.

Pictures drawn for the grid

Every picture is drawn directly on the grid, up to 30×30 cells — not borrowed from an icon set, which lost its character once the holes were filled in. Each picture has a strong outline, because the detail inside is what disappears. The shapes are hand-approved one by one before they enter the library.

One way to put it together

Before a Mosaic can be exported, the Builder checks that the pieces fit together only one way and give the right picture. If they could go together another way, it tries another position, or prints a coordinate on one piece as a reference. No two pictures in a game look alike.

Straight answers

Before you decide.

Do I lose anything if I say no?
No. Your Builder works exactly as it does today, with letter games, hint cards, host guide and the full print set. PRO is an addition, not something that is taken away.
Are the Mosaic pictures AI-generated or from an icon pack?
Neither. Every picture is drawn directly on the grid for this product, and approved by hand before it is added to the library. The story prompt is given the list of pictures available for your theme, so the AI chooses from real pictures instead of inventing one.
Could players put the pieces together the wrong way?
The Builder checks that the pieces fit together only one way and give the right picture. If they don’t, it tries another position for the picture, and if it still can’t it prints a coordinate on one piece to act as a reference.
Does Hard mode really have no coordinates?
Correct. Easy and Medium print each piece’s position; Hard leaves them off and players match the outlines themselves. Pieces never need rotating — each says “this way up”.
Can I use Mosaic with the other features in the Builder?
Yes. A Mosaic chapter still earns a word that goes into the Final Lock, hint cards and the host guide still apply, and games can be saved and re-exported like any other.
